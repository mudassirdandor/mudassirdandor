## 5. Business-Problem-First Services Framework

To ensure maximum relevance, all solutions are structured around actual organizational pain points rather than technical tools. Technology acts as the helper, never the focal point.

```text
[CLIENT'S BUSINESS PROBLEM]
           │
           ▼
[CUSTOM BUSINESS SOLUTION]
           │
           ▼
[TECHNOLOGY STACK USED]
```

### Services Mapping:

1.  **Google Workspace Automation**
    *   *Business Problem:* "We waste hours every week manually copying data from emails into Google Sheets, sending PDF certificates, and updating registers."
    *   *Business Solution:* Custom workflow automation that triggers custom email alerts, syncs spreadsheets automatically, generates PDFs from forms, and connects separate cloud systems.
    *   *Technology Used:* Google Apps Script, Google Sheets, Gmail API, Google Forms, and secure API webhooks.
2.  **Local SEO & Maps Visibility**
    *   *Business Problem:* "Our physical branches do not appear when local customers search for our services on Google Maps, and our reviews are unmanaged."
    *   *Business Solution:* Location optimization and search visibility audits. Aligns citations, manages coordinate placements, tracks review metrics, and builds customer feedback dashboards to drive local discovery.
    *   *Technology Used:* Google Business Profile integrations, Local SEO practices, Google Maps, and review analysis.
3.  **Business Intelligence & Dashboards**
    *   *Business Problem:* "We have years of transactional data stored across files, but we cannot see our daily sales or metrics in one clear view."
    *   *Business Solution:* Clean, interactive performance dashboards that structure raw database views into clear trends and geographical summaries.
    *   *Technology Used:* Power BI, Excel dashboards, SQL database views, and clean data modeling.
4.  **AI & Chatbot Assistants**
    *   *Business Problem:* "Our support desk is overwhelmed with basic, repetitive intake questions from visitors."
    *   *Business Solution:* Intelligent, automated intake assistants that answer FAQs, gather contact details, and automatically log inquiries into spreadsheets.
    *   *Technology Used:* Dialogflow NLP, Gemini API (with secure backend proxying), and web chat modules.
5.  **Interactive Web Applications**
    *   *Business Problem:* "We need a secure, custom portal where clients can register for events, complete assessments, and view their status."
    *   *Business Solution:* Fast, secure, and mobile-friendly web portals featuring clear, multi-step forms and instant verification.
    *   *Technology Used:* React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and local browser state caching.
