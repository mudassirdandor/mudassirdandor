# Project Overview

## Project Name
**mudassirdandor Platform** — Mudassir Javed Digital Headquarters & Executive Decision Intelligence Platform.

## Purpose of the Portfolio
A professional digital storefront and authoritative platform for Mudassir Javed, specializing in Business Intelligence (BI), Data Analytics, AI Automation, and Local Business Intelligence.

## Primary Audience
- Enterprise Executives & Decision Makers
- Growth Product Managers
- Business Owners & Local Retail Client Groups

## Website Goals
- **Authority**: Establish top-tier expert credibility in decision intelligence.
- **Evidence-Based Conversion**: Convert visitors into consulting leads through high-impact, quantifiable case studies.
- **Frictionless Lead Acquisition**: Route high-intent inquiries straight to a streamlined CRM/Google Sheet dashboard.

## Technology Stack
- **Frontend**: React 19, Vite, Tailwind CSS (v4), TypeScript
- **Animations**: Motion
- **Backend/Integrations**: Express (Vite Middleware Proxy), custom Google Apps Script APIs

## Current Project Status
V6 Reshaping Phase — Restructuring layouts, locking in design rules, and organizing structured evidence-based sections for production readiness.

## Homepage Structure
Single-page consulting landing page incorporating a visual hero, trusted brand anchors, problem-solution frameworks, proof grids, and direct contact gateways.

## Long-Term Vision
Transition into a comprehensive self-contained Decision-Support Ecosystem, hosting direct analytics dashboards, sandboxes, and automated report-generation utility tools.
