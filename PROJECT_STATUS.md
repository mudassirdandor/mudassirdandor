## 8. DESIGN DECISIONS

1.  **Professional Positioning:** Mudassir Javed is consistently positioned as a **Digital Business Solutions Consultant** representing all core areas of competence (AI, BI, Data, Web, Google Workspace, Local SEO).
2.  **Human-First Psychology:** The homepage prioritized Mudassir's profile, qualifications, and personal story before technical visualizers or dashboards.
3.  **Business-Problem-First Flow:** Service mapping follows a strict `Business Problem → Custom Business Solution → Chosen Technology` sequence.
4.  **Google Workspace Elevation:** Google Apps Script, Sheets, Forms, and Gmail automation are promoted to first-class, front-row services.
5.  **Local SEO Terminology:** "Local Business Intelligence" is described in customer-friendly terms: Google Business Profile, Local SEO, and Google Maps Visibility.
6.  **Trust-Building Pillars:** Credibility is supported by his MSc in Statistics, active GitHub contributions, and 40+ professional certifications.
7.  **Light Editorial Aesthetics:** Clean, high-contrast, off-white background layers using Space Grotesk headings and JetBrains Mono status tags.
